Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really guarantee a first interview?
Yes. With our Premium program, we guarantee you’ll get at least one real job interview or client conversation within the program duration.
How is my CV turned into a website?
You share your CV, projects and goals. Our team builds a clean, professional personal website that presents your experience, skills and proof of work — with your own name in the web address — so anyone can find and trust you.
How do you rank my website on Google?
Your Premium expert applies SEO and digital marketing to your website: the right keywords for your name and role, optimised titles and descriptions, structured content, fast mobile pages, and links from your LinkedIn, GitHub and other profiles. This pushes your site up when employers and clients search for you.
What kind of expert guidance will I get?
1:1 guidance from an expert who helps with your personal brand, LinkedIn growth, outreach and applications. You also get access to group coaching, a dedicated outreach CRM, and our company and client networks.
Who is this program for?
Students, fresh graduates and early-career professionals — and freelancers looking for their first clients — who have the skills but aren’t getting noticed, shortlisted or called back.
How long does the program take?
The program runs as a structured cohort. Your website is built first, then optimised and promoted, and outreach continues until you land your first interview or client conversation. Exact cohort dates are shared when you apply.
